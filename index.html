◆ § 06 · Pool — Mint · TV-01

Pool & rules

Set in the contract at deploy. Nothing changes after.

◆

The first print is free for every Binance Agentic ID.

◆

90% of paid BNB goes to the LP reserve. 10% goes to the project wallet.

◆

Max 10 prints per Agentic ID — first one free, the rest 0.002 BNB each.

◆

The pool opens itself at print #10,000. The LP is burned, so it can never be pulled.

◆

211,000 prints max, ever. Printing continues after the pool opens, near the pool price.

◆

2% tax on buys and sells — 1% burned, 1% to marketing. Printing and normal transfers: 0%.

Is the first print free?

Yes. Every Binance Agentic ID gets one free print — you only pay the small network gas fee. A free print pays 40% of the stage’s coin value.

What do I need to print?

A wallet on BNB Chain and a Binance Agentic ID. One ID can print up to 10 times.

When can I trade $TALV?

At print #10,000 the pool opens on its own with the saved BNB, and the LP is burned. From then on, anyone can buy and sell.

How do I get a second free print?

Invite 3 friends and wait for each of them to complete their first print. Your second print is free.

Why do later coins pay less?

Each stage holds 2,000 prints and pays a little less than the one before. Printing early pays more — every friend brings the next tier closer.
